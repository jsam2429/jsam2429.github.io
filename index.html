  1. Gym Sim

    A Roblox gym game. Train individual muscle groups, qualify on physique, then compete on stage in a posing skill-check for prize money. The server holds every piece of authority and the clients only send intent, so the training maths stays pure enough to unit-test outside Studio.

    Luau, Rojo, Wally, ProfileStore, Lune

    26 August – 21 October 2026, 160 commits

  2. JobDecoder

    Paste a job-listing URL and get back one scannable report: a verdict on whether the role is worth applying for, a salary benchmark, and a list of red and green flags. Every claim shows its source, its date, and how confident it is. Extraction is best-effort and never takes the report down.

    Next.js 16, TypeScript, Claude, Prisma, Neon Postgres

    22 July – 12 August 2026, 40 commits

  3. ReelClaw Templates

    Reusable templates for short-form video. Hand a generator script some clips and a hook line and it returns a render-ready 1080 × 1920 reel, captions sized automatically and kept inside the safe zones that TikTok and Reels crop to.

    Node, HyperFrames, ffmpeg, headless Chrome

    29 July – 7 August 2026, 11 commits

  4. PropertyReport

    Enter any Victorian address and get a property report built from authoritative government data: crime rates, school catchments, flood and bushfire overlays, demographics. Datasets are ingested offline and committed, so a request never waits on a flaky government endpoint. Finished reports cache to a link you can share.

    Next.js 16, TypeScript, Prisma, Neon Postgres, shadcn/ui

    4 June – 7 July 2026, 117 commits

  5. AdLens

    An ad-trends dashboard. It ingests advertising creative from Meta, TikTok, and Google, enriches each one with Gemini, and surfaces the themes, formats, and volume leaders moving in a given industry. Three layers that talk to each other only through Postgres.

    Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Gemini, Vitest

    20 May – 3 June 2026, 31 commits

  6. Wander

    A travel planner. Choose the kinds of places you care about and how far you are willing to go, and it lays out a day with the transport between each stop worked out.

    React, Vite

    April 2026

  7. Life OS

    One app in place of three: habit streaks, goals broken into milestones, a Pomodoro study mode that tracks subjects, and a friends feed so progress is visible to someone other than you. A mobile app and a web app sharing one type-safe API.

    Expo, React Native, Next.js, tRPC, Prisma, Supabase

    March 2026

  8. MemeMerge

    A mobile merge game, built in Unity for iOS and Android and wired up with the whole live-ops layer: interstitial, rewarded and banner ads under a COPPA-safe configuration, anonymous sign-in on first launch, and cloud leaderboards.

    Unity, C#, Google AdMob, Unity Gaming Services

    March 2026, 9 commits

  9. Iron Log

    A workout logger. Save templates, run a session against a rest timer, and watch volume per muscle group trend across months. React on the front, Express and SQLite behind token auth.

    React, Vite, Express, SQLite, JWT, Recharts

    March 2026
